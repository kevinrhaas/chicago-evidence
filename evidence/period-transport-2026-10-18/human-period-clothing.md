2026-10-18. User requests generic adult teamsters/people at photographic quality: driving wagons/carriages, riding, standing near transport and walking streets. This report records concrete existing assets and source observations. Application/model/queue files were read only; this is the sole new file. ## Actually read early Chicago clothing text

The local newspaper transcriptions provide dated commodity vocabulary, not pattern measurements. These are OCR/transcription readings, **not page-image verification**. They are useful acquisition locators and lower-confidence bounds on an original reconstruction; corrupted words/numbers remain unresolved.

- `chicago/chicago/reference/newspapers/Transcriptions/Chicago_Democrat_1833-11_to_1835-08/Chicago_Democrat_1833-12-17_Vol1_No4_Transcription.txt`: advertisement block lists “Bangup Cord”, ready-made clothing of different kinds, boots/shoes, with an ad date November 26, 1833. This supports locally marketed workaday clothing/material vocabulary, not that any particular teamster wore it.
- Same directory, `Chicago_Democrat_1834-07-02_VolI_No31_Transcription.txt`: W. Kimball ad lists fine fur hats and palm-leaf hats plus ready-made clothing. Another goods list includes woollen/Canton flannels, gloves/hosiery and palm-leaf hats. These are actual text reads; complete ad boundaries/date must be checked in original columns before a source-level claim is registered. Palm leaf is a period hat option, not a mandate to dress everybody identically.
- `chicago/chicago/reference/newspapers/Transcriptions/Chicago_American_1835-06_to_1835-08/Chicago_American_1835-07-04_Vol1_No5_Transcription.docx`: the extracted ad text lists black bombazine frock coats/vests, cassimere and London-drill pantaloons, moleskin hunting coats, round jackets, shirts/collars/stocks and other goods, with many OCR spelling errors. July4 is **after** the first scene's July1 target: corroborating nearby trade vocabulary is not proof every article was stocked before the scene. A June8 issue carries a similar clothing block but is more heavily damaged. The July18 issue extraction identifies Tuthill King beside similar apparel vocabulary; do not silently transfer the seller attribution to an uncertain earlier block without page collation.

The nineteenth-century printed newspaper text is public domain by age; the modern transcription container's licensing and accuracy were not separately established here. Cite the underlying dated paper and acquire page images before deriving fine garment geometry, lettering or a factual inventory. Do not claim OCR mistakes are actual period garment names.

**What this supports for a generic 1835 work adult:** an originally authored, explicitly reconstructed short jacket/round-jacket versus longer coat choice, separate shirt and waist layer, trousers/pantaloons and a dated hat alternative. Exact pattern, fit, colors, cloth weight, buttons, suspenders, footwear and wear distribution still need sources or declared bounded reconstruction. A modern shirt/jeans/cowboy-hat kit is not justified by these texts.

## Concrete production acceptance for the requested examples

The current user direction authorizes work on generic adults and these transport demonstrations. Preserve the distinction between this requested scope and the existing blanket L1 implementation flag: record any necessary scoped policy/contract change explicitly in the app review instead of silently dropping review flags or pretending today's code already allows scene figures. Named/culturally specific depictions remain separate; do not ask for the same generic-work authorization again.

1. **Two wardrobe families first:** generic work teamster and formal carriage-service adult, each date-bounded. Author an anonymous adult body with original or clearly licensed anatomical/head references, not a borrowed named person's face. Record asset license and per-garment source IDs, inferred/reconstructed details and scene intervals; get actual garment multiview/cut/seam references, not just a general street photograph.
2. **Photographic body/material gate:** plausible anatomy at head, hands, neck and ankles; articulated fingers; skin/eye/teeth/hair with consistent physically based response; no primitive joints showing through clothes. Close-up neutral-light turntables and reference-registered views must expose remaining mismatch. Texture size or passing GLB validation does not demonstrate realism. Source-owned historical likenesses are not necessary for a generic adult, but all anatomy/texture inputs still need reuse rights.
3. **Clothing gate:** model construction rather than painted-on clothing: date-appropriate silhouette, layer overlap, seams, closures, collar/cuffs, garment thickness, footwear and hat fit. Cloth weave stays at metric scale. Distinguish material from dirt; work wear is bounded reconstruction, not a universal dirty-worker stereotype. Retain monochrome color uncertainty. Seated garments must deform without exposing body intersections or rigid coat skirts.
4. **Five actual demonstrations using the same portable master:** wagon driver on box, carriage driver on distinct seat, mounted rider on fitted saddle, standing by horse/carriage, and walking street specimen. Each needs an independently reviewed pose/clip; `sit`/`walk` naming alone is not content. Driver's hands bind rein endpoints, feet to footboard; rider pelvis to saddle, feet to stirrups, hands to reins. Riding gear differs from driving harness. Record target transforms/contact error over motion and varied body size rather than forcing everyone into one pose.
5. **Motion/interaction gate:** physical hand grip, seated balance, rein handling, gait-synchronized rider motion, transitions and grounded feet without sliding. Horse/mount/vehicle collision envelopes tested together; no claim of boarding or riding controls merely because a posed person is parented to a seat.
6. **Portable/export/performance gate:** keep contract skeleton/name/frame and in-place clips; validate skin/morphs/materials, master/web derivative parity, all four LODs and mobile/desktop image/error checks. Measure actual new adult costs and combined horse+gear+vehicle+driver cost. Existing primitive fixture budgets and passing transport gates are not the new photographic character's acceptance. Distant animation/LOD must retain an intelligible seated or walking silhouette.

## Required embedding proof, distinct from topology

A single connected Euler1 disk proves topology only. Before bridging, validate original common chart is globally injective over affected triangles, both boundary loops simple and nonintersecting, actual inner/outer domain relation, and no projected boundary touches. Consistent signed triangle areas alone do not exclude nonlocal overlap. Test all nonadjacent projected triangle/edge pairs and original-vs-refined partition coverage. Do not polar-project loops to an ideal ellipse or linearly interpolate differently shaped loops without proving each intermediate strip is injective: that was the lip failure mechanism. A twisted cyclic offset can preserve manifold incidence while intersecting its bridge.

If a single common projection cannot represent both skull hole and retained ear boundary as nonoverlapping nested domains, stop this annulus method and report actual obstruction; do not switch projection pertriangle until positive areas appear. A3D bridge can exist without a single planar graph, but then needs explicit3D triangulation/embedding proof rather than pretending a chart proof applies. Retained ear front/back cup geometry can be nonconvex and close;18 matching vertices alone is not enough.

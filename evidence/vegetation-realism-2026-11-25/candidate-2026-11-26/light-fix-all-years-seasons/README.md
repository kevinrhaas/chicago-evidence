# Better Trees: every year and season

The complete published-browser sweep covers 1812, 1835, 1871 and 1904; spring, summer, fall and winter; Light, Balanced and Full; phone and desktop. All 96 cases completed with zero browser errors. Twelve bounded bodies retain their source-before/source-after, exact image and metadata hashes, complete logs, and night/confidence transitions. Screenshots and raw metadata are beside their receipts.

The measured source is the full working tree `48f1bb2124e3f95e9262baf10b87ce1c020722e5`. These are original executions at that source, before the later inherited student-data UI integration; they are not relabelled as an execution on a newer tree. The later complete combined source has separate Native, calibration and committed-head qualification.

The two Water Tower legacy controls use all owned pre-season Core renderer modules on the same assets and data. Their summer control retains the old scene's actual 8 October 1871 date; compare with the new **fall** captures, also 8 October. The control does not support the new 1871 seasonal choices. No planting records are deleted, and these images do not attest historical tree placement.

Known visual limitation: the 1904 winter backyard has very dark building faces. The same appearance is visible in the earlier pre-Light seasonal capture retained under `m2-current-seasonal-views/m2-current-flora-seasons/after-1904-desktop-balanced-winter-back_yard.jpg`; this comparison does not establish when that building-lighting issue originated. The images are preserved without claiming universal photorealism. Software-renderer DPR1 timings here are diagnostic, not calibrated phone performance or hardware FPS.

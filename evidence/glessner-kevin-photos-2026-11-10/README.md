# Glessner House, Kevin's own photographs, 2026-11-10

Twenty photographs of the Glessner house (1800 S. Prairie Avenue) taken by Kevin Haas
(project owner) on 2026-11-10, in the Prairie Avenue door, along 18th Street, in the alley
and from the former 1808 (Keith) lot. Supplied in the project thread on 2026-11-12 as the
reference for ticket T-3564. Credit: photographs by Kevin Haas, 2026. Files are the uploads
as received (iPhone JPEGs, EXIF kept), renamed for what they show.

These are modern views. Modern fittings in them (the accessible ramp and its rail, the
museum signs, the bird spikes, the "No ball playing" sign, the bins and the truck) are not
part of the 1904 scene and are excluded from the model.

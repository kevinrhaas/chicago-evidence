## Source and date boundaries

The source is `kurz_allison_1893`, public-domain retrospective panel 5, read directly from `data/sources/assets/prefire_views_kevin_2026_08/p3_1.png`. It shows the small capped drum on the right-hand corner blockhouse. It provides appearance, never coordinates or metres. The 1893 artist's caption does not turn the vignette into a contemporary 1835 construction record.

This is the second fort rebuilt in 1816, not either first-fort blockhouse. Whistler's 1808 observation that cupolas were not yet built concerns the first fort at that date; it does not prohibit later inferred presence, and it does not attest the second-fort cupola.

The first-fort and second-fort images and record identities must remain separate. The 1856 Kellogg image named `chicago_fort_dearborn_river_mouth_1830.jpg` actually depicts roughly 1836–1849 (Lake House visible), and the 1838 harbor view also postdates the target scene. The two 1831 views repeat one Wau-Bun composition rather than independent testimony. Peck's 1892 painting is retrospective.

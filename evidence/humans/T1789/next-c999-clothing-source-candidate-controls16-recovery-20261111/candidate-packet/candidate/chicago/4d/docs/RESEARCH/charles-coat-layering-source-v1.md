# Charles coat layering: unbaked source candidate

The owner reports that Charles's trousers come outside his coat while walking.
The saved selected-published v8 walking close image also shows a skin-coloured
opening at the centre waist below the buttons. Its six originals are retained
with seven native request failures: these images are research, not fit/photo
qualification. Their new raw WEB inputs match the woven-v1 8660b207/86a5fdfa
children. The 84.441-second four-GLB fixture measured assets and saved no images.

The original Seely coachman and mourning photographs (1902, public domain;
source `seely_1902_coachman_livery_plates`) show a continuous closed front through
the waist. Reference JPEGs 87b0e309/854bbc32 are restored byte-exact from evidence
e63363d; no colour, Nelson likeness or Chicago outfit is inferred from them.
Existing dark green and all dimensions remain reconstructed graphical choices.

The existing shared gentleman skirt encloses the bare body, omits a front wedge
from its standing construction test, and follows a heuristic leg-weight field.
Charles's separately built breeches add 10 mm ease and up to 14 mm thigh fullness.
The shared bare-body test therefore does not certify the trouser layer, and no
standing radial envelope proves walking triangle clearance.

This candidate changes only the coachman construction. It builds the unchanged
breeches first, encloses both actual source layers, uses one closed angular ring
with seam corner UVs instead of overlapping/coplanar front sheets, and samples
the actual breeches triangle skin field with an 80 mm blend to the bodice seam.
The 40 mm correspondence height band, 150 mm distance bound and 2.5% maximum
discarded influence mass are declared candidate engineering parameters, not
period measurements or fitted acceptance tolerances. Excessive/missing matches
refuse rather than silently falling back to guessed thigh weights.

The inherited >4 mm sampled radial construction constraint is retained as a
diagnostic over the layered source envelope, including the front. It is NOT
minimum distance to actual emitted triangles. Matching interpolated weights is
also NOT a deformation guarantee: LBS of interpolated bind positions/weights
generally differs from barycentric interpolation of an actually posed triangle.
The small synthetic controls deliberately retain this counterexample. Actual
anatomy construction, native exported LODs, walking/idle full-cycle triangle
crossings, edge/face contacts, minimum gaps, front coverage/self-contact and
runtime-variation cases are all UNRUN. Photography and broader T-1789 stay open.

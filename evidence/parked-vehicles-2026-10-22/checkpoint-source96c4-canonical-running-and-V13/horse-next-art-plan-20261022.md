1. **Choose and freeze the anatomical source.** A properly sculpted/scanned licensed base takes priority. Otherwise the next meaningful task is an organic sculpt against the cleared exterior and anatomy references, rather than another tube-profile edit. Review head, shoulder, joints and hooves in neutral clay. Keep the current study as a frame/landmark comparison. Do not unwrap and bake an anatomy version that is about to be replaced.

2. **Author UVs and maps in a suitable offline art workflow.** Preserve a canonical editable master and a reproducible qualified export/bake recipe; this runner needs no new DCC installation. Put seams in less visible medial/ventral regions, keep head/eyelid/hoof texel density deliberate, and allow extra padding for mip levels. Share maps across mirrored regions only where it does not duplicate conspicuous markings or reverse hair flow. Emit `TEXCOORD_0` and a consistent MikkTSpace tangent basis with handedness; split UV/tangent seam vertices correctly instead of corrupting normals.

   Author a plain bay coat from original paint/groom inputs or an explicitly cleared texture package. Base color is sRGB; tangent normal and packed AO/roughness/metallic are linear, with roughness inG/metallic inB. Keep dielectric metalness0. Do not bake lighting, shadows or highlights into color, and do not use high-frequency noise as a substitute for anatomical planes. Short coat needs coherent hair-flow directions and subtle roughness/normal variation; muzzle, eyelids and hoof wall need distinct tissue response. The CC0 modern photographs and PD plates guide anatomy only here; unknown-rights historic photographs remain non-derivative references. Retain exact texture sources, edits, licenses and hashes.

3. **Build the groom as hair, not another solid envelope.** Restore the anatomical neck crest and tail dock/core where the temporary masses displaced skin. Author groom curves/clumps, then derive a restrained set of layered, tapered cards or ribbons with original baked color/alpha and normal/roughness atlases. Give mane direction and tail cross-sectional volume, irregular ends and root-to-tip variation. Start with standard glTF `alphaMode: MASK`; test cutoff, mip fringes, depth/shadows and card normals. Use double-sided cards only where necessary. Do not depend on anisotropy or other material extensions before portable renderer support is verified. Intentional open groom surfaces need separate topology/root-clearance checks; the closed body still needs its full manifold/intersection proof. Do not silently reuse the old whole-mesh single-component assertions or waive all groom intersections.

4. **Extend the PN-only export path deliberately.** The portable asset needs actual UV/tangent accessors, images/textures/samplers and PBR/alpha references; the existing flat-material writer does not provide them. Preserve metres/+Y up/+Z forward/+X left and native root datum. A four-material layout—body including muzzle/inner ear via atlas, eyes, hooves, shared mane/tail—can stay inside the existing six-draw ceiling. Retain the60k/25k/8k/2k body LOD ceilings and4K/2K/1K/512 edge ceilings as measured engineering limits, not mandatory map sizes. Keep4K editable masters offline. A first2K body color/normal/packed-map plus two1K groom-map runtime layout is about74.7MiB as RGBA8 with full mips, before compression and other overhead. Measure actual GPU formats, decoded memory and near-view quality before increasing resolution; do not load duplicate4K map sets by default.

5. **Review the actual exported art before installation.** First preserve the exact four baseline whole-horse cameras/lights/native frame, then add explicitly declared close head, hoof and groom views and neutral-light rotations. Inspect strand silhouette, alpha/shadow stability, coat direction, UV seams, eye/wet-tissue response and hoof planes at desktop/mobile near/mid/far distances. Compare maps-on and clay views so finish cannot conceal anatomy. Validate texture color spaces/tangents, glTF buffers and rights; measure bytes, draws and decoded texture/frame costs on the actual portable output. No camera fitting, glossy lighting trick or successful structural check substitutes for photographic acceptance.

The useful parallel split after the source is frozen is **UV/coat materials** and **mane/tail grooming**, followed by one integrated static-art review. Rig/tack/animation remain later work. Until the anatomical source is settled, preserve this plan and the procedural evidence instead of spending the next cycle on tiny eyelid parameters or another solid-tail rebuild.

## Producer paths that can change the material GLB

- apply_materials.py:29-48 converts exact source JSON to float64, computes covariance/PCA through np.linalg.eigh, chooses/sign-normalizes grain/transverse axes, computes UVs, solves texture derivatives and normalizes tangent frames. BLAS/LAPACK implementation, eigenvalue ties, reduction order and CPU math can alter UV/tangent bytes or accessor extrema. There is a sign rule but no canonical basis/tie rule for a repeated eigenvalue. Cheap read-only covariance inspection of the exact reviewed158-part capture found44 records with near-repeated diagonal variances, including stake bolts and bench squareheads. Rings/hubs mostly use explicit alternate UV rules. This is a concrete ambiguity risk, not proof that it caused CI failure.
- generate_tiles.py uses NumPy RNG, leggauss(4/8), vectorized sin/exp/powers/reductions and SciPy erf before integer pixel quantization. Seed identity alone does not guarantee all these implementation-dependent math outputs are bit-identical across dependency/platform builds. leggauss itself also depends on numerical eigensolvers.
- Pillow Image.save(compress_level=9) embeds nine PNG byte streams directly into the material GLB. Pillow/libz changes can change encoded bytes even when decoded RGB is identical. Pixel equality and encoded-PNG equality must be measured separately.
- apply_materials np.linalg.svd only contributes anisotropy to the separate study receipt; that value does not enter the GLB. make_tiles floating summary statistics also live in its separate receipt. These alone cannot explain0138 parent differences. Their underlying arrays or encoded PNGs can.
- refine_materials has not processed the finish recipe at this failing point, so its finish recipe hash, finish textures and final274e assertion are not yet the cause. Covered-core mean/unit/dot arithmetic is later still.

# Prairie Avenue child and teen review prototypes

Canonical build: [42997832738](https://github.com/kevinrhaas/chicago/actions/runs/42997832738), source `a229ee241fc1fd756f2be5b8a5190910e1ba52c5`.

All five figures exported at four raw and four compressed detail levels, with animation audits passing. Forty browser inspections (five roles, four detail levels, desktop and phone) passed the mechanical checks. These checks do not establish photographic acceptance.

The town captures use the published renderer, with temporary overlays to load the actual exported figures and give five existing child cards their age-appropriate bodies and hair. The Glessner entrance is a review staging location, not a claim that all five children lived there. `runtime-files.json` binds the renderer and data; `town-preview.json` binds the exported assets, overlays, cameras and screenshots.

The period photographs show other children. Clothes, colouring and individual appearance are reconstructed. The toddler comparison uses an older girl; the teen girl's comparison uses eleven-year-old Ethel Roosevelt. Neither is a same-age likeness reference. The Ethel photograph was found after construction and is a review reference, not a geometry source.

No owner approval, photographic acceptance or scene activation is claimed. The original source photographs and their provenance are in the parent folder.

# Chicago and Pine: research for a future utility corner

The public-works report lists one new hydrant at the northeast corner of historic Chicago Avenue and Pine Street during the year ending April 1, 1871. The next entry, at the Gund Engine House, is a separate installation. This is a reporting-year list of new hydrants, not a complete census. Carrying the corner installation forward to October 8 is a continuity inference; casting, dimensions, finish, orientation, exact curb offset and operating condition remain unresolved.

The report also documents the adopted practice of putting street names on city lamps. It does not establish a lamp or label at this corner, nor its design or lettering. The inspected Pine Street photograph is a separate period reference lead, with no readable street name or securely identified corner hydrant. Its restricted CHM image bytes are excluded. The original report title page gives a Cambridge, Massachusetts imprint; the packet retains the exact bibliographic evidence and scan attribution rather than inventing a CC0 license.

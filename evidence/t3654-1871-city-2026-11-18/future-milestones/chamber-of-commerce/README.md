# Chamber of Commerce: research for a future milestone

This read-only packet concerns the original pre-fire Chamber of Commerce at LaSalle and Washington. It is research for a later named-building milestone; no model, solved camera, repository asset-license record or image-gate acceptance was produced. It does not change the current generic city baseline or its validation status.

Only the original 23-file public whitelist and the separate 15-file followup whitelist were copied, plus their manifests. The CHM poster, diagnostic wrong-page downloads and the complete 216-page book are excluded. `initial-packet/` retains the inspected Andreas pages, source records, rights metadata and handoff. `contemporary-followup/` retains three complete source pages and a three-page PDF extract, provenance, rights records and the unresolved comparison.

The sources also disagree about hall dimensions and window counts. These reports are preserved independently; no hybrid room or adjudicated height is asserted. The one-foot depth disagreement is separate from map-registration uncertainty. An original 1865 newspaper page was not acquired in this bounded followup; the secondary transcription remains a lead rather than verified absolute-height evidence.

The lithograph on PDF page 8 is the same composition as the CHM item but was independently acquired from the University of Michigan/HathiTrust scan hosted by Commons. The scan retains its margins, source labels and public-domain watermark. The file-specific Commons record supplies PD-scan/PD-old-70 and Public Domain Mark grounds; this is not a CC0 claim or permission for the unrelated CHM digital copy. The linked HathiTrust policy could not be read through the web tool, and that limitation remains explicit. The three-page extraction preserves source image streams; original whole-book SHA256 and page mapping are recorded without archiving the full book.

The 1866–67 catalogue date is retained. The secondary January 1866 attribution is not visibly printed on these pages. Signboards, pictured people and vehicles do not establish tenancy, sign survival or activity on October 8, 1871; those require appropriately dated records. Use the packet as a reference candidate and discrepancy record for the next research and image-gate pass.

DELIVERED
A standalone engine-neutral JSON recipe, source records, deterministic Python emitter,
self-contained glTF2 GLB and generated sidecar. The model is 6,597,140 bytes, 74,228
triangles, 143,354 vertices and 9 material primitives, with three embedded original
512px procedural textures. It is not registered in the app and has no world position.

The north exterior has five upper archheads in 2/1/2, a wider central tripartite window
with three upper lights, three arched portals, paired lower windows, a high basement,
quoins, masonry courses, projecting cornice, mansard, four smaller roof oculi and central
oculus/crest. West has ten upper arch bays, lower openings and a provisional side
entrance. A bare staff, chimneys, front steps and bounded north/west basement railings
are present. Unpictured east and brick rear faces are explicitly reconstructed.
There are no interiors, crowds, street-life scenes or ancillary annexes.

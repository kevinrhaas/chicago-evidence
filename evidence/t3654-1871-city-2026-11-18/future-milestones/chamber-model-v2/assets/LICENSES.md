## Reference-image public-domain route

References are in `../references/`, not embedded in the model. The independent Chicago
Illustrated scan is attributed to the University of Michigan/HathiTrust, carried by
Wikimedia Commons; its file record declares PD-scan, PD-old-70 and Public Domain Mark1.0.
The original was published in the U.S. in1866–67. The complete page and Public Domain
watermark are preserved. This is not the museum's restricted digital poster.

The Andreas image is a scan of an1885U.S.publication, public domain by age, supplied by
Internet Archive. Its inspected title date and original-publication rights basis are
recorded; no new CC0 or provider-license assertion is made. The existing project's
ANDREAS2_COMMERCE source accepts the public-domain-original route. This candidate
records the same route for review before registration. Internet Archive's item had no
explicit rights/license metadata; current general provider terms were not verified.

These references may be compared to the candidate under their stated public-domain
basis. Their drawings are evidence, not textures or surveys. No restricted CHM bytes
are present, including in the public archive whitelist. Each retained source file is
hashed in `data/sources/` and the final archive manifest.

V2 adds original parameterized stone relief, shafts, capitals, balusters and urns.
Their source-visible forms derive from the two cleared publications; carving
profiles, casing depths and surface colors remain explicitly reconstructed. The
portal shadow proxy is a closed exterior representation, not a modeled interior.

V2 also retains the complete John Carbutt stereo card, NYPL digital ID
`G90F172_009F`, downloaded independently through Wikimedia Commons. The Commons
file record explicitly declares `PD-US` and `PD-scan (PD-US)`, a mechanical scan of a
public-domain original. Credit: John Carbutt; New York Public Library, Robert N.
Dennis collection of stereoscopic views, Photography Collection, Miriam and Ira D.
Wallach Division of Art, Prints and Photographs. Exact file/revision URLs and hashes
are in `data/sources/carbutt_chamber_nypl_G90F172_009F.json`. The full card, printed
attribution and both stereo halves remain intact. The linked current NYPL search
returned 403; current NYPL item terms were not independently inspected. The catalog
date `1865?–1885?` is kept distinct from visual identification of the original
building phase. Only the pale central roof roundel face is newly inferred from this
photo; no image pixels become a texture and no exact dial marks or time are asserted.

# Source-visible form corrections for v2

Both full cleared images were directly inspected, followed by the original-pixel coordinate viewports in `source-form-detail-coordinates.png`. The largest shared omission is the **continuous central roof apron with concave shoulders and urn-topped end pedestals**. Build it around the central oculus and connect it to the center-bay cornice; floating rings do not reproduce the pictured form.

The **upper arch crowns** should be much taller and more complex. Approximate central glazing-apex/crown readings are y1840–1845/y1800–1808 in Andreas and y488–493/y460–466 in the lithograph. Relative to the pictured reported-height aperture, a reconstructed central crown rise around **2.1–2.5m** is a useful trial. V1's roughly0.72m pin is visibly too small. Add nested moldings, a stepped/flared keystone and shouldered or curled sides. Flanking crowns are smaller, around1.4–1.8m as a bounded trial. Keep the central aperture unchanged.

The **entrance arcade** needs round shafts, pronounced capitals and bases, outer square pilasters and angular spandrel relief. Shaft width appears roughly one sixth of a clear opening; the current2.5m opening suggests0.35–0.5m diameter. Dark passages should not be crossed by prominent bright office-window bars. The lithograph's people obscure the bases; Andreas is clearer.

The **central upper sill** projects as a balcony-like base with short balustrade across the center bay. A reconstructed0.65–1.0m projection and0.65–0.9m balustrade height establishes the large visible form before detailed tuning. Smaller roof oculi need molded casings connected back to the roof, and the top roof edge needs a layered crest/dentil profile.

Pixel locators, trial bounds and limitations are in `form-refinement-audit.json`. These are bounded reconstruction suggestions, not new surveys or solved camera coordinates. Do not change plan dimensions or overall height to disguise ornament/camera discrepancies. No native cameras, models, app files or frozen v1 files were changed by this audit.

Identity/card first, depicted actor second
- tools/compile_scene.py:1074 (scene_people_manifest), :1147 (compile_people),
  and :3355 use the scene's own household records and emit its people sidecar.
  For later scenes the convention is data/residents_<scene>/households/.
  Source IDs must resolve under data/sources/, and any home/workplace claiming
  a structure must refer to a structure actually standing in that scene.
  A context building alone must not be silently treated as a sourced structure
  identity. A historical street number remains source text until period mapping
  supports its location; do not modern-geocode 75 Huron.
- A canonical directory row uses id, name, household/file, grade, occupation,
  present, lives_at/works_at where supported. Its person record carries sources,
  note and per-field confidence. Do not invent a household composition, spouse,
  relationship, age, expanded initials or arrival date just to fill this pattern.

  The generic census image-link helper is tailored to census fields, so do not

Portable instance and appearance
- Authority: docs/HUMAN-ASSET-CONTRACT.md sections 10-11 and
  data/humans/human_instance.schema.json. The actor lives at
  data/humans/instances/1871/hi_<person_id>_1871.json, indexed by that directory's
  index.json, after the canonical person exists. No second person directory is
  created by an instance. Named interaction opens resident_card, not role_card.
- Fields separate appearance.asset_id/variants; placement.local_e/local_n in
  metres, heading_deg and terrain/walk_surface anchor; animation.clip/loop/phase;
  behaviour; interaction.selectable/radius_m/opens; lod; display; review_required;
  and provenance.grade/basis/sources/liberty/seed/replaceable_by.
- The person's existence/occupation and the depiction have separate grades.
  Body, apparent age, face, complexion, hair, height, clothing cut/color/wear,
  tools, stance, gait and exact position are reconstructed unless independently
  supported. A cleared generic body is an engineering ingredient, not a likeness.
  Record the bounded choices under liberties; no invented dialogue presented as
  evidence. Each asset needs license provenance in assets/LICENSES.md; source
  check_required is insufficient for deriving an asset.
- Useful existing example: data/humans/instances/1835/hi_peck_philip_1835.json
  separates an attested merchant from a reconstructed body, clothes, time and
  storefront placement. Reuse the contract, not Peck's identity or 1835 wardrobe.
- The shared portable-human pipeline uses canonical masters, c4d_humanoid_v1,
  glTF/JSON provenance and LOD exports. The T1789 library README explicitly calls
  its modular male workwear a source checkpoint, not photographic acceptance.
  Existing source tests or old model pictures cannot qualify a new 1871 outfit.

Sunday 16:00 activity/route boundary
- renderers/web/js/human-days.js implements behaviour.presence and
  behaviour.day[{dates?, chance?, note?, spans}]. Dates support ISO from/to,
  months and years; this schema has no weekday field. A date-bounded October 8
  variant can state its Sunday assumption explicitly instead of inheriting a
  generic weekday routine. Spans cover 0-24 local solar hours.
- Each span has from/to/indoors; an indoor span names where; an outdoor span
  names at {local_e,local_n,heading_deg}. activity may be stand/talk/work/walk.
  A walk uses to_point and optional via/once, work can use spots, and doing is
  the short action phrase exposed on the card/harness. These are staged choices,
  not an archival itinerary. Doors and an indoor Ask interaction also require
  a real dated building/door record, not an invented entrance.
- Do not infer that a directory-listed theatre carpenter was working that Sunday,
  at home, carrying a saw, supervising staff, attending a performance, or taking
  a specific street route. Any such choice must be separately reconstructed.
  The directory does not identify his working hours or exact October presence.
- Important runtime behavior: human-days.js:37-72 can turn a lone stand into
  small chore motions, gather nearby people, and add breaks. A minimal pose is
  therefore not necessarily inert. Review the actual activity produced before
  describing it as a limited reconstruction; avoid weekday-store templates.
- route.js uses dated streets/walks, footprints, water and bridge decks; it
  explicitly rejects importing a modern grid into earlier scenes. Walking
  prefers walks, with collision-safe approaches. human-days.js routes between
  spans, while handwritten via points still represent chosen routes. Validate
  future positions against the 1871 ground and crossings, not today's address
  numbering. No route or coordinates are proposed in this handoff.

## Decision

**Best concrete reusable-model lead: BlueMesh's `HORSE [DRAFT HORSE]`, Sketchfab `725065392aad4b04b7a172f8cd965497`.** Its acquired 1920×1080 preview has substantially more coherent anatomy than the current Lyndon/OGA family: shoulder flows into chest and barrel, shaped croup, anatomically differentiated joints, volumetric muzzle/nostrils and eyelids, flared hoof walls and hair rather than a solid mane slab. Its public model API expressly supplies CC BY 4.0. This is a promising basis, not an acquired mesh: the normal anonymous download API returned **401 Unauthorized**. No account was created, auth circumvented, viewer geometry extracted or asset payload guessed. Root can preserve this as an exact acquisition lead; work can proceed now through the original rebuild below using already acquired CC0/PD anatomy references. Do not repeatedly optimize materials on the rejected coarse silhouette.

BlueMesh describes it as a draft horse and still in progress. That uploader title neither proves a historical breed nor makes it the correct first light-harness body. The stronger geometry is worth evaluating if legitimately acquired; shape, topology, textures, unseen surfaces, metric scale and actual licensed package contents remain unverified. Foreground pose is slightly elevated rather than a complete straight side view; feathering hides some fetlock shape. No canonical model should be selected solely from this thumbnail.

## Concrete original remodeling handoff: proceed with static horse first

1. **Replace the skin mesh, retaining no rejected tube-leg silhouette as the final topology.** Start one contiguous bilateral body surface with shoulder/scapular volume flowing through pectorals into barrel, withers into neck, and pelvis/croup into upper hind limbs. The Seton skeleton fixes fore/hind segment order; the plates and CC0 photos ground surface continuity. Establish a landmark table for poll, orbit, nostril, mouth corner, withers, shoulder point, elbow, carpus, fetlock, pastern/coronary band, stifle, hock, hip point, tail root. Mark each as observed anatomical relationship or reconstructed dimension.
2. **Keep the first body's medium light-harness proposal explicit.** Retain the spec's 1.55 m withers as a *chosen reconstructed target*, not a measurement extracted from either modern photograph. Body length/breadth are likewise design bounds pending calibrated reference. Do not average the two modern animals into a purported historical breed. Front, far side, belly, joints hidden by grass, and exact cross-sections remain original reconstruction.
3. **Build actual limb structure and joint shapes.** Fore: shoulder/upper arm/elbow under trunk → forearm → carpus → slim cannon with posterior tendon relief → fetlock → oblique pastern → hoof. Hind: femur/stifle forward under flank → tibia → backward-projecting hock → cannon → fetlock/pastern/hoof. Use longitudinal connected loops and explicit joint volumes, with nonuniform cross sections. Do not append cylinders at disconnected ball joints. Hoof walls taper from coronary band to a broader bearing rim; model heel bulbs and a simple sole/frog underneath rather than a uniform black cone. Shoe is a removable later period-sourced assembly.
4. **Rebuild head as eye socket/cheek/jaw/muzzle volumes.** Fit eyelids to the eyeball; add lacrimal corner and ear thickness/cup. Make each nostril a rimmed cavity with an interior wall/dark occlusion, not a polygonal dark decal. Use a closed relaxed lip seam with a real mouth corner; avoid an open black portal. Underjaw and throatlatch must read from front/quarter as well as side. Plate 6 directly grounds the visible side, while underside/far side remain reconstructed.
5. **Author new mane, forelock and tail geometry.** Scalp/crest and tail dock are attachment surfaces; create overlapping tapered clumps/cards that follow the observed general hair direction. Vary silhouettes and ensure roots embed in the skin. No modern braid, clipped mane, historical coat pattern or breed-specific feathering is imported as attested. Use original neutral coat textures, not lighting/color sampled as historic truth from B&W images. Modern CC0 photos may ground short-hair roughness/flow, but the production manifest must identify any pixels actually reused.
6. **Freeze static topology/UVs before any gait work.** Keep eyes, mane/tail and hoof material slots separate; no saddle/bridle/harness or people in the first acceptance view. Ship a neutral standing pose with four verified contacts. Once acceptable static shape exists, generate the engine-neutral metre/Y-up/+Z derivative and retain the authored master and source manifest. Later rig chains follow these landmarks; do not adopt the human rig or untested third-party animations.
7. **Use fixed diagnostic views that expose failure.** Later authorized bake: full side, front, rear and both quarters in neutral light, then close head/hoof/joint views. Compare with the acquired reference pixels only for the relationships each shows; camera/pose registration and source uncertainty must be saved. Clay render first, coat after shape. Stop/remodel if joints float, lower limbs read as tubes, hooves lack heel/coronary structure, or head openings look geometric. No more long SAT/material fit loop on the rejected body. A static specimen can remain unpublished while these visible defects are corrected.
